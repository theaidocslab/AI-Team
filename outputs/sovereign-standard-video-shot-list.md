## Before You Hit Record — Checklist
- [ ] Confirm the chat widget is answering with real Groq responses (ask it something unscripted first, off-camera, to be sure it's not misfiring)
- [ ] Confirm the lead form's Netlify notification email is arriving (test-submit once before filming so you're not guessing on camera)
- [ ] Clear browser history/tabs before recording
- [ ] Have your real name/email ready to type into the lead form live — using an obviously fake one on camera undercuts the "this actually works" proof beat

## What Not To Say On Camera
Don't repeat the page's "Only 3 spots available... price increases to $997 soon" line unless you've confirmed it's a real, trackable constraint (like the old page's "5 builds a week," which was a disclosed, honest limit). Saying an unverified scarcity claim out loud, on camera, is a bigger credibility risk than leaving it off — if it's ever checked and doesn't hold up, it undercuts everything else in the video that IS real proof.
