## Executive Summary
This concept works because it sells something coaches and consultants are already quietly losing money over — a growth system they can run on command instead of rebuilding from scratch every time revenue dips. The market isn't short on AI content; it's short on anyone showing the actual machinery instead of a prompt list. That's the opening.

The risk is looking like one more "here's my AI stack" video. The fix is proof, not polish: every agent's raw output stays on screen, uncut, and the handoff — one agent's file becoming the next agent's input — is the single moment the entire video has to earn. That handoff isn't a demo flourish. It's the whole argument for why this is a system worth paying to install, not a prompt worth screenshotting. Every choice below — script, offer, lead magnet, sequence — exists to protect that one moment on screen.

## Why The Topic Should Work
Solo coaches and consultants watching this already have proof they're good at the work — client results, testimonials, a calendar that fills some months. What they don't have is a way to reproduce that pipeline without personally becoming the strategist, copywriter, and funnel builder every single time. That's the nerve this hits — not "AI is confusing," but "I am the entire growth department, and I have no repeatable process to run when the pipeline goes quiet."

This video doesn't teach AI literacy. It hands over the exact system that ends that scramble, built and proven in one sitting instead of promised for later. The offer path is already built into the video's structure, not bolted on afterward. The one thing standing between this and "another AI demo" is discipline: show the handoff, don't describe it.

## The Unique Mechanism
**The Agent Relay Framework** — five specialist agents that each do one job and hand a structured artifact to the next, mirroring how a real growth team divides labor:

1. **Market Signal Researcher** — pulls demand signals and audience psychology for the business idea.
2. **Offer Architect** — converts the signal into a specific, priced, mechanism-backed offer.
3. **Content Strategist** — turns the offer into a content angle built to sell it.
4. **Lead Magnet Builder** — designs the exact free asset that filters for buyers.
5. **Follow-Up Sequencer** — writes the sequence that converts the lead into the paid offer.

The believability lever is the *handoff*: agent 2's output must visibly be built from agent 1's file on screen. That structural proof is what separates "system" from "chatbot trick" — and it's the single most important thing the video has to show.
