# Lead Scoring Template — The AI Docs Lab™

A manual research tool. You (or whoever's doing prospecting) fill this in
while actually looking at a real business's website, search results, and
socials. Nothing here is auto-generated — every score has to trace back to
something you actually saw.

Pairs with `lead-scoring-template.csv` — a fillable spreadsheet version of
the same rubric you can open in Google Sheets or Excel.

## Who to look for

- Coaches (life, business, career) with under 5,000 followers
- Contractors/trades (plumbers, electricians, renovators) in small-to-mid US cities
- Solo consultants, insurance agents, small law practices

Priority order: Salt Lake City / Wasatch Front first, then expand
nationally.

## The three scores

### 1. Website Score (0–10)

| Score | What you see |
|---|---|
| 0 | No website at all — just a Facebook/Instagram page, or nothing findable |
| 3 | A website exists but is broken, unstyled, or clearly abandoned (dead links, no HTTPS, last copyright year is old) |
| 5 | A dated but functional site — looks like a 2015-era template, no mobile optimization, generic stock photos |
| 8 | A modern-looking site, but no clear call-to-action or way to actually book/apply |
| 10 | A modern, mobile-friendly site with a clear CTA already |

Lower score = bigger opportunity for us. A 0–5 is a strong candidate.

### 2. Search Visibility Score (0–10)

Search **"[their service] near [their city]"** (e.g., "life coach near
Ogden UT") in an incognito/private browser window.

| Score | What you see |
|---|---|
| 0 | They don't appear anywhere in the first 2 pages of results |
| 3 | They appear on page 2 only |
| 6 | They appear on page 1, but below the map pack / below 3+ competitors |
| 10 | They rank in the map pack or top 3 organic results |

Lower score = bigger opportunity. Record **who did show up instead** — you
need this for the opening line.

### 3. AI/Lead-Capture Score (0–10)

Look at their actual site.

| Score | What you see |
|---|---|
| 0 | No chatbot, no lead form, no booking link — just a phone number or "contact us" mailto |
| 4 | A generic contact form only (name/email/message, no urgency, no offer) |
| 7 | A booking calendar (Calendly, etc.) but no chatbot |
| 10 | Already has a chatbot or AI-driven lead capture |

Lower score = bigger opportunity.

## Opportunity Score

**Opportunity Score = 10 − average of the three scores above, rounded to
the nearest whole number.**

Example: Website 3, Search Visibility 0, AI/Lead-Capture 0 → average 1 →
Opportunity Score 9.

Flag anything **7 or higher**.

## Opening line rule

One sentence, built only from what you actually found. No generic pitches,
no assumptions. Format:

> "I searched '[service] in [city]' and didn't see [Business Name] in the
> results — here's what showed up instead: [competitor / nothing]."

Or, if the gap is the lack of a booking/chat tool instead of visibility:

> "I checked [Business Name]'s site — looks like the only way to reach you
> is a contact form. No booking calendar, no way to ask a quick question
> after hours."

If you can't point to something specific and verifiable you actually saw,
don't send it — go find another lead instead of guessing.

## Workflow

1. Find a business (LinkedIn, Facebook group, Google Maps, local directory — manually, by hand).
2. Open their website (or confirm they don't have one).
3. Run the "[service] near [city]" search in a private window.
4. Score all three categories in the CSV.
5. If Opportunity Score is 7+, write the one-line opener.
6. Leave the outreach itself to a human — this template only finds and
   drafts.
