Purpose: get coaches/consultants to `demo.theaidocslab.com` today, ahead of the full proof video.

and the real, disclosed urgency lever (Dr. Eric personally builds every page, 5/week cap) —

Use when messaging a coach/consultant directly. Personalize the bracketed line — a DM that's
obviously copy-pasted gets ignored by this audience faster than most.

> Hey [Name] — saw [something specific: a post, their site, a mutual]. Quick one, no pitch:
> does your website actually get you clients, or is it more of a digital business card?
>
> I built an AI-powered landing page for a coach — chatbot trained on the business,
> built to actually get found by ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity, not just Google. You can go
> mess with the live example right now, no email, no form: demo.theaidocslab.com
>
> If it's useful, the real version is $297 once, not a monthly funnel bill. If not, no worries —
> just thought it was worth a look.

**Follow-up if no response after ~4 days:**
> No pressure at all — just bumping this in case it got buried. The demo's still live if
> you want to poke at it: demo.theaidocslab.com

## Where To Post First (fastest reach, zero ad spend)

1. **Coach/consultant Facebook groups and LinkedIn communities you're already in** — post the LinkedIn copy above, adapted to each group's rules on self-promotion.
2. **Direct DMs to coaches you already follow or have interacted with** — the cold DM script, personalized.
3. **Your own LinkedIn/IG feed** — the organic posts above.
4. **Reply/comment on coach content** (not spam — genuine value-add comment, then DM if there's a real fit) rather than cold-pitching in public comments.

## Notes
- Every asset routes to the **demo**, never straight to the application — matches the sales plan's core fix (proof before commitment).
- Keep the "5 builds/week" language exactly as written — it's the plan's real, disclosed urgency lever, not manufactured scarcity.
- Swap in a coaching-niche proof example (chatbot Q&A, AI-search result) the moment one exists — until then, lead with the chatbot demo itself as proof, since visitors can test it live.
