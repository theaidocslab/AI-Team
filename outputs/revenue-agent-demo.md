## The Unique Mechanism
**The Agent Relay Framework** — five specialist agents that each do one job and hand a structured artifact to the next, mirroring how a real growth team divides labor:

1. **Market Signal Researcher** — pulls demand signals and audience psychology for the business idea.
2. **Offer Architect** — converts the signal into a specific, priced, mechanism-backed offer.
3. **Content Strategist** — turns the offer into a content angle built to sell it.
4. **Lead Magnet Builder** — designs the exact free asset that filters for buyers.
5. **Follow-Up Sequencer** — writes the sequence that converts the lead into the paid offer.

The believability lever is the *handoff*: agent 2's output must visibly be built from agent 1's file on screen. That structural proof is what separates "system" from "chatbot trick" — and it's the single most important thing the video has to show.

## What The Creator Must Show On Screen To Make This Credible
- The literal output at every agent step — real text, not paraphrased summaries.
- The handoff moment itself: zoom on the exact line where Agent 1's output gets pasted into Agent 2's prompt, repeated at every transition (2→3, 3→4, 4→5).
- The opportunity score / structured data as a clean on-screen callout, not scrolling text.
- The actual downloadable lead magnet file opening on screen, not just described.
- A visible timestamp or build-time counter, and an explicit acknowledgment of any editing/compression — protects credibility instead of risking a "staged" reaction.
- One explicit spoken line: "the money is in the system, not the tools."

## What To Remove Because It Feels Generic
- Any framing of Claude Code as a "coding tool" — it must be introduced purely as a business system from the first mention.
- Generic "AI can help you save time" language anywhere in the script or emails.
- Describing the lead magnet as "bonus resources" — it must always be framed as the literal agent files from the video, not a content upgrade.
- Vague workshop pricing left unresolved into email 5 — pick and state one price/tier before the pitch goes out.
- Any mention of the done-for-you premium tier before the workshop email — it belongs after workshop completion, not in the free sequence, or it reads as a jump from $0 to five figures with nothing in between.
