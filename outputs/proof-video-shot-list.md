# Proof Video — Ready-to-Film Shot List

Runtime target: **~4:30**. Film this in order — it's built to be shot mostly in one take with screen recordings cut in, so you don't need heavy editing to make it work.

## Title
**"I Let ChatGPT Find My Client's Website (Watch This)"**

Backups if you want to test alternatives: "Stop Paying $300/Month For A Landing Page That's Invisible To ChatGPT" · "This Coach's Website Shows Up In ChatGPT — Here's Proof" · "I Built A Client A Website In 48 Hours. Then I Tested It." · "If You Want Clients To Find You On ChatGPT, Build This First"

Thumbnail text options: "IT ANSWERED" · "ChatGPT Found It" · "$297 vs $400/mo" · "Proof, Not Promises" · "Watch It Work"

---

## 0:00–0:20 — Hook
**Camera:** face on camera, direct to lens. No logo intro, no "in this video."

**Say word for word:**
> "If you're a coach and your website has never actually gotten you a client — this isn't another 'here's what you should do' video. I'm going to show you a real page, built for a real coach, and then I'm going to ask ChatGPT to find her. Live. No script. If this doesn't convince you, nothing will — so just watch."

---

## 0:20–0:50 — The Problem
**Camera:** still on you.

**Say (use this pain language verbatim — it's pulled straight from your own application form, so it's already proven language for this audience):**
> "You get traffic and nobody books. Or your page exists and generates zero leads. Or you're 100% on referrals and it's terrifying. So I built one, live, in 48 hours. Let me show you."

## 0:50–1:30 — Show The Real Page

## 1:30–2:15 — Live Chatbot Demo (unscripted)
**Screen:** click the chat bubble open. Type a real, unrehearsed question character by character — don't paste it in, typing on screen reads as real. Suggested question: *"Do you work with people who've never done coaching before?"*

**Let the answer generate on screen with no cuts** — this has to feel unedited to land as proof.

**Voiceover after it answers:**
> "That's not scripted. That's a real AI chatbot, trained on this business, answering a real question."

---

## 2:15–3:00 — Live AI-Search Demo (your strongest proof beat)
**Screen:** open a new, visibly blank browser tab (empty history reinforces "not staged"). Go to Gemini. Type on screen: *"who does 3PL warehousing at the Freeport Center"* (or your current best real example — swap in a coaching-niche client here the moment you have one live).

Let it surface **Oborn Transfer & Storage** on screen. Click through to `oborn.com`, scroll to the footer, and hold on The AI Docs Lab's credit for a full 2–3 seconds so it can actually be read.

**Say on camera:**
> "This is a real client site, this is a real AI recommendation — and yes, it's a logistics company, not a coach, because that's who I built this one for. The same system is what gets built for you, in your niche. This works because of the AI crawler plugin built into every page — it's what gets you found by ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity, not just Google."

*(Naming the logistics/coaching mismatch honestly, out loud, is stronger than hoping nobody notices — it reads as more credible to a skeptical audience.)*

## 3:00–3:20 — NEW: Build Speed, Shown Not Claimed
**Screen:** a sped-up or timestamped clip of an actual build session — even 10–15 seconds of fast-forwarded screen recording with a visible timer or on-screen clock counting up works. The goal is to visually back up "under 30 minutes," not just say it.

**Say on camera (voiceover over the sped-up footage):**
> "People assume '48 hours' means I'm slow. I'm not — once I have everything I need from someone, the actual build takes under 30 minutes. The 48 hours is me reading the application and thinking through how to position them, not sitting at a keyboard."

*(This is the beat that makes the "5 builds a week" limit on your page believable instead of suspicious — it proves the cap is a choice about quality, not a bottleneck you're hiding.)*

## Short-Form Cutdowns (once the full video is done)
Cut these four standalone clips from the same footage — no new filming needed:

1. **Chatbot proof clip** (1:30–2:15) — strongest standalone hook for Reels/TikTok: "I asked a real AI chatbot a question, live."
2. **AI-search proof clip** (2:15–3:00) — best for cold audiences who've never heard the AI-search-discoverability angle before; nobody else is making this claim.
3. **Build speed + cost comparison clip** (3:00–3:50) — good for retargeting warm viewers who've already seen the proof and just need the price/trust close.
4. **Hook + problem clip** (0:00–0:50) — works as a standalone pattern-interrupt for coaches scrolling, with the dropdown-language pain points as on-screen text overlay.
