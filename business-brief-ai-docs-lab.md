# Business Brief — The AI Docs Lab (Coach Landing Page Offer)

Creator:
The AI Docs Lab™ (Dr. Eric), theaidocslab.com

Audience:
Coaches, consultants, and service professionals (the intake form also welcomes adjacent niches like real estate, family law, home services) who have real expertise and client results but no landing page, or a landing page that isn't generating leads.

Product:
A custom, AI-powered landing page delivered in 48 hours: custom landing page + conversion copywriting + a 24/7 AI chatbot trained on their business + a lead capture form + booking integration + optimization for discovery by AI search tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity), not just Google. Includes a free domain and the first 30 days of hosting.

Price & mechanism:
$297 one-time build fee, no monthly software fee, buyer owns it forever. After the first 30 days, hosting is optional at $10/mo (pure server cost, no lock-in — buyer can host anywhere). Positioned against two alternatives: agencies charging $5,000+, and funnel platforms charging $97-297/mo for the page builder plus a separate ~$97/mo chatbot add-on ($300-400+/mo forever), neither of which build for AI-search discovery.

Funnel:
Not an instant checkout. Visitors fill out a detailed application (contact info, niche, ideal client, main offer & price, biggest result they get clients, biggest lead-gen problem, booking link, notes). Dr. Eric reviews every application personally within 24 hours, confirms the spot, and delivers the page within 48 hours of the confirmation call. Guarantee: if he doesn't believe he can build a page that converts, he says so and refunds instantly.

Existing asset:
The live application landing page already exists (see reference/coach-landing-page-application.html) — premium black-and-gold design, direct/no-fluff copy, "why so cheap" and "already paying for a funnel platform" objection-handling sections built in, soft urgency ("this is your chance") without fake countdown-clock gimmicks.

Task for the agent team:
The page itself already exists and converts visitors who reach it into applications. The open problem is getting more of the right people (coaches/consultants) to actually land on this page and apply. Build a plan for driving qualified traffic/applications to this exact page, plus what should happen after someone applies.

Voice:
Direct, confident, premium, no fluff, no hype-y AI buzzwords, proof over promises — matches the existing page's tone exactly.

Constraints:
- Do not suggest reskinning the page or changing the price/offer — the product and page are fixed as described.
- Do not suggest fake scarcity or countdown timers — the brand explicitly rejects that.
- Marketing angles must speak to coaches specifically, not "any small business."
- Keep every recommendation tied to getting a qualified coach to actually submit the application.
